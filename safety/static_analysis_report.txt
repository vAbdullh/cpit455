  [5] SA010 — FALSE POSITIVE (justified)  (Severity: LOW)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Location : viewstudents.jsp, insertstudent.jsp (failover catch blocks)
  Finding  : Catching generic Exception considered too broad.
  Decision : These catch blocks are the top-level fail-safe for the entire page.

           This is a deliberate safety pattern, not a coding error.
